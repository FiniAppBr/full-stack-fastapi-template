## Research Summary

Key Findings (LlamaIndex, NVIDIA 2024, Chroma Research):
- Optimal chunk size: 400-512 tokens for factoid queries
- "More is not always better" - too many chunks saturates context
- Context Rot: LLM performance degrades as input grows
- "Chunking and retrieval strategies are far more impactful than model power"

Sources:
- https://www.llamaindex.ai/blog/evaluating-the-ideal-chunk-size-for-a-rag-system-using-llamaindex-6207e5d3fec5
- https://research.trychroma.com/context-rot
- https://www.databricks.com/blog/long-context-rag-performance-llms
