ConnectAI - Value Proposition Validation
=========================================

## What problem are we solving?

Businesses want AI to handle customer conversations on WhatsApp/Instagram/messaging apps.

Current options:
1. Hire humans - Expensive, slow to scale, inconsistent
2. Use chatbots (Chatfuel/Manychat) - Rigid button flows, not conversational
3. Use pure AI (ChatGPT plugins) - No control, unpredictable, hallucinates
4. Build custom (Rasa/Botpress) - Requires developers, weeks of work

## What do businesses actually need?

Talk to 10-20 businesses using WhatsApp for customer service.

Ask them:
- What do you currently use for customer messages?
- What frustrates you about it?
- How many conversations per day?
- What % could be automated?
- What would make you switch tools?
- What features are must-have vs nice-to-have?

## Hypotheses to validate:

1. Businesses want AI that can handle natural conversations (not rigid buttons)
2. They need AI to access their knowledge (FAQs, policies, product info)
3. They need AI to DO things (book appointments, process orders, etc)
4. They need AI to know when to hand off to humans
5. They want control without coding

## What's our unique value?

Why would someone use ConnectAI instead of:
- Just using ChatGPT API directly?
- Using Voiceflow/Botpress?
- Hiring VAs?

We need a clear answer.

## Questions we can't answer yet:

- Do businesses actually want to "design" their AI's behavior?
- Or do they just want it to "work" out of the box?
- Is visual configuration better than just text instructions?
- Do they care about conversation "structure" or just outcomes?
- Are we solving a real problem or creating complexity?

## Next steps:

1. Talk to 10 businesses that use WhatsApp for customer service
2. Understand their current pain points
3. Build the SIMPLEST possible solution that addresses those pains
4. Test if they'll actually use it
5. THEN decide on features/UX

Don't build features based on what seems cool.
Build what solves a validated problem.
