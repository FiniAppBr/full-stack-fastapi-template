ConnectAI - Real-World Use Cases (Natural User Requests)
================================================================

These are simulated but realistic scenarios of how businesses would
actually describe what they need when they come to use ConnectAI.


USE CASE 1: Law Firm (Boutique Immigration Law)
------------------------------------------------
"We get like 50 WhatsApp messages a day asking the same stuff - 'how much
does a visa cost', 'what documents do I need', 'can you help with my case'.

I want the AI to:
- Know our prices for different visa types (we have a PDF price list)
- Know what documents are needed for each procedure
- Book initial consultations with our 3 lawyers (check their Google Calendar)
- NEVER give legal advice, just information about our services
- If someone asks a specific legal question, say 'I need to connect you with
  one of our lawyers' and notify us on Slack
- Be professional, no emojis, we're a law firm not a pizza place
- Speak Portuguese and English
- If someone seems frustrated or uses angry words, immediately hand off to human

Basically I want it to handle the boring repetitive questions so my
paralegal can focus on actual case work instead of answering 'how much is
a work visa' for the 10th time today."


USE CASE 2: Dental Clinic (3 locations)
----------------------------------------
"Ok so we have 3 clinics in different neighborhoods. People message asking:
- Is there availability this week?
- How much does a cleaning cost?
- Do you accept my insurance plan?
- I need to cancel my appointment

What I need:
- AI checks availability across all 3 locations (we use Calendly)
- Knows our price table (cleaning = R$200, filling = R$300-500, etc)
- Has the list of insurance plans we accept (I can give you a document)
- Can cancel/reschedule appointments
- If someone has a dental emergency (pain, broken tooth), mark as URGENT
  and send me a notification immediately
- Friendly tone, can use some emojis, we want to be approachable
- If someone asks about a complex procedure (implants, braces), offer to
  schedule a free evaluation
- Send confirmation messages 24h before appointments
- After appointment, send care instructions (we have templates for each procedure)

The goal is my receptionist stops spending 3 hours a day on WhatsApp and
can actually focus on patients who are here in person."

USE CASE 4: Restaurant (Family-owned Italian)
----------------------------------------------
"We get WhatsApp messages all day:
- Do you have a table for 4 tonight at 8pm?
- What's on the menu?
- Do you deliver?
- Are you open on Sunday?
- I'm allergic to gluten, what can I eat?

What I need is simple:
- Check reservations (we use a Google Sheet, low-tech I know)
- Send our menu (PDF with photos)
- Tell people we DON'T deliver, only pickup or dine-in
- Our hours (closed Mondays, open Tue-Sun 12pm-10pm)

  say 'I'm so sorry, let me get the owner to speak with you'

My daughter handles the phone and WhatsApp now and she's tired. She wants
to be in the kitchen learning to cook, not glued to her phone."


USE CASE 5: Real Estate Agency (Mid-size, 20 agents)
----------------------------------------------------
"We have 20 agents and like 150 properties listed. People message asking:
- Do you have a 2-bedroom apartment in [neighborhood] under R$2000?
- Is [specific property] still available?
- Can I schedule a visit?
- What are the requirements to rent?

I want the AI to:
- Search our property database (we can integrate via API or upload a CSV
  that updates daily)
- Filter by neighborhood, price, bedrooms, etc
- Send photos and details of matching properties
- Check which agent is responsible for that property
- Schedule visits by checking that agent's calendar
- Explain rental requirements (proof of income, guarantor, etc - we have
  a standard document)
- If someone wants to make an offer or negotiate price, connect them
  with the specific agent
- Professional but not robotic - real estate is about relationships
- No emojis, keep it business
- If someone asks about buying (we only do rentals), refer them to our
  partner agency

Right now every agent handles their own WhatsApp and people message the
main company number and we have to manually forward to the right agent.
It's chaos. I want one AI that handles initial contact and routes properly."

USE CASE 7: Accounting Firm (Small business focused)
----------------------------------------------------
"We serve small businesses - restaurants, shops, freelancers. Messages:
- How much do you charge?
- Can you help me with [specific tax situation]?
- I need to send you documents, where?
- When is the deadline for [some tax thing]?
- My previous accountant screwed up, can you fix it?

What the AI should do:
- Explain our pricing (we charge monthly based on revenue bands, I can
  give you the table)
- For specific tax questions: don't answer! Say 'I'll connect you with
  one of our accountants for a free consultation' and schedule a call
- Documents: send instructions on how to upload to our portal (link + simple steps)
- Deadlines: we have a tax calendar, the AI should know important dates
- For people with problems from previous accountants: empathetic tone,
  schedule an urgent consultation, flag it for me
- Professional, clear, no jargon
- Absolutely no emojis, we're accountants
- If someone asks 'can you hide income to avoid tax' or anything shady,
  politely decline and don't continue the conversation
- Explain we do bookkeeping, tax filing, payroll - give brief description
  of each service

Accountants are expensive per hour, so if the AI can handle basic info
and qualification, my team only talks to serious leads. That's what I want."

PATTERNS OBSERVED:
------------------
1. Most want AI to handle repetitive questions, humans for complex/emotional
2. Tone varies wildly by industry (lawyer vs gym vs pet grooming)
3. Almost everyone wants calendar/scheduling integration
4. Price transparency is huge - people want to know costs upfront
5. Qualification is key - don't waste human time on bad-fit leads
6. Emoji usage is polarizing - industry dependent
7. Handoff triggers are specific: anger, complexity, sales closing, complaints
8. Most want to upload documents/knowledge base rather than configure rules
9. Integration needs: calendar, spreadsheets, existing booking systems, Slack
10. Audio messages for accessibility (mentioned by law firm for older clients)

CONFIGURATION COMPLEXITY:
------------------------
Simple setup (under 15 min):
- Pet grooming, restaurant, gym, cleaning service
- Upload price list, connect calendar, set tone, done

Medium setup (30-60 min):
- Courses, designer, photographer, tech repair
- Need portfolio links, package explanations, qualification logic

Complex setup (2+ hours):
- Real estate (property database integration)
- Accounting (tax calendar, compliance requirements)
- Law firm (multiple professionals, complex procedures)

WHAT THEY DON'T MENTION:
-----------------------
- No one says "I want to design a flowchart"
- No one says "I want to configure nodes and edges"
- They describe OUTCOMES and BEHAVIORS, not architecture
- They think in terms of "what should it say when X happens"
- They want to give the AI documents and have it "just know"

IMPLICATION:
-----------
The interface should probably be:
1. "What does your business do?" (text description)
2. "Upload your knowledge" (PDFs, price lists, FAQs)
3. "Set your tone" (professional/friendly/energetic + emoji yes/no)
4. "When should AI hand off to human?" (checkboxes for common triggers)
5. "Connect your tools" (calendar, booking system, etc)

NOT:
- "Design your conversation flow"
- "Create decision nodes"
- "Map your logic tree"

The flowchart might be something that power users discover later, or
something that's auto-generated from the simpler configuration.
