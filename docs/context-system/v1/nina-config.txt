STAGE TRANSITIONS (derived from state)
--------------------------------------

conexao → descoberta
  when: customer responded with any engagement

descoberta → validacao
  when: skill_level != null (customer shared their level)

validacao → apresentacao
  when: interest_level in ["warm", "hot"]

apresentacao → prova_social
  when: presented method (auto after apresentacao response)

prova_social → intencao
  when: showed social proof (auto after prova_social response)

intencao → fechamento
  when: intent == "ready_to_buy" OR customer says "quero", "sim", "me passa"

intencao → nutricao
  when: intent == "objection" AND objection not resolved after 2 attempts

fechamento → boasvindas
  when: intent == "purchased"

nutricao → intencao
  when: re-engaged after content sent
